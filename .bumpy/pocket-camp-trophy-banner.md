A banner by the Pocket Camp's fire marks a Trophy reached while you play.

For an hour after the Crew reaches a Trophy during your session (a Crew goal, a dungeon or raid cleared, or a Rare slain), a small banner stands behind the Pocket Camp's fire. Its tooltip lists each one and how long ago, newest first, and a click on it opens the Trophies tab. Trophies reached while you were away don't raise it.
