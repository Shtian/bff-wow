Several Crews on one ruleset and faction

You can now be in more than one Crew on the same ruleset and faction, and play with one at a time. Each Character remembers its Crew, and a new Character asks who it's playing with. Switch from the Crew menu at the top of the panel, or from Settings → Your Crews. The other Crews see you as offline, not as having left. You can give each Crew your own name, and every Crew you leave is kept until you delete it, so you can rejoin it later.
