The Dungeons tab is easier to read at a glance.

A dungeon's bar now covers exactly its levels, with its name running on past it when it's longer, and dungeons you've outlevelled sink back. A line runs down from your level through the dungeons, every other ten levels is shaded, and hovering the line says how many dungeons fit that level. With the Camp on, your friends stand on the line as their Camp heads. The overview at the bottom is now a small map of the whole line.
