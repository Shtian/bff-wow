Seasons in the Camp: autumn, the lunar new year, Midsummer

Three seasons on top of winter. Each section puts one choice side by side; the bar picks what the other sections use. Bats and fireworks are rare by design: the buttons play them now.

Autumn · 18 Oct – 1 Nov

1 · The pumpkin

Decor, placed like the tree: after everyone and after the tent, on the roomier side, on the ground. Lit inside, flickering on the fire's tick. Lit on the fire's side and mirrored on the fire's right.

2 · Bats

One or two crossing the sky in about 4 s, flapping in 2 frames, high over the names. Under labels.

3 · The fire

Today's flame frames, tinted. Turn on the goal flare in the bar to check it still plays over the orange fire.

Lunar new year · 22 Jan – 12 Feb

4 · Paper lanterns

A sagging string across the upper rows, red and gold lanterns gently swaying, none over the fire, where the firework goes. Drawn only where the lowest lantern clears every name and hat.

5 · The firework

Every 30 to 60 s, over the fire. It reuses the max-level burst (seven sparks flying out, sparks then embers, 1.8 s), in red and gold. Here next to the burst it reuses.

Midsummer · 20 Jun – 5 Jul

6 · The bonfire

Turn on the goal flare in the bar: the flare replaces the flame frames, as it does today.

7 · The ribbon pole

Decor, placed like the tree. The ribbons flutter in 2 frames on every third tick.

8 · Fireflies

Yellow-green pixels drifting slowly and blinking, kept clear of the fire and of names. Under labels.
