Winter in the Camp

Snow, a small tree and lights on the tent, from 15 Dec to 2 Jan. Each section puts one choice side by side; the bar picks what the other sections use.

1 · Snow

Caps on the ground, the tent's roof and each bedroll's top edge, nothing on figures. Flakes are single pixels behind everything but the backdrop, a step every 2 ticks, drifting a column sideways every 5 steps, and never over the fire. Names are drawn over them.

2 · The tree

About 10 by 14, lit on the fire's side and mirrored on the fire's right, as the tent is. Placed after everyone and after the tent, on the side with more room, so it's the first thing dropped.

3 · Lights on the tent

Five coloured pixels on the roof. The bare tree's lights follow the same pattern.
