Day and night in the Camp

The Camp was drawn as a night scene against a dark window. These are four day looks, three suns, three moons and two kinds of star, each in the panel's band, While you were away and the Pocket Camp. Names and the window under the band are there so you can judge contrast and how bright the band reads over the dark panel.

1 · The day look

Sky, hills, pines, grass and the fire's weaker light on it, with the sun from the bar. Tonight's scene first, for reference. The window under the panel's band is lit by the fire as it is today; While you were away's isn't.

2 · Winter by day

Each look's own snow on the ground, solid away from the fire and dithered into its glow, with the tree, the caps, the tent's lights and the flakes. The flakes are today's sprite: check they still read against a pale sky.

3 · The sun

Close up on each look's sky, then in the band with the look, place and season from the bar.

4 · The moon and the stars

Over tonight's backdrop, which already has its baked-in stars. The new stars are the few that twinkle, slowly (each over 5 to 8 s).

5 · Where they sit

Each place in the lunar new year, where the lanterns hang across the top, at the narrowest band, While you were away and the widest. By day the sun; by night the moon, stars and the firework over the fire (the bar's "Firework now" plays it). The readout says what the sun or moon runs into.

6 · Together, with your picks

Phase in the bar: day, night, or flipping every 10 s to see the instant swap at a redraw.
